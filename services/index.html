05

Search & AI Visibility

Help search engines understand your website and prepare useful information for search and AI-driven discovery.

  • Technical SEO — Review crawlability, canonical URLs, redirects and the technical structure of the website.
  • On-page SEO — Align page titles, descriptions, headings and readable content with the page purpose.
  • Search Visibility — Make your services and key pages easier for search engines to discover and understand.
  • AEO — Structure clear, useful information for answer-oriented search experiences.
  • GEO — Improve technical and content readiness for generative discovery without promising AI citations.
  • Structured Data — Connect factual organization, founder, service, page and project information.
  • Sitemap / Robots / Indexing Foundations — Set up canonical sitemap coverage, crawler rules and indexability checks.
  • AI-search Readiness — Keep important information crawlable, factual and consistently structured.
  • Bing / IndexNow Readiness — Prepare safe discovery and notification foundations; submissions follow approved production publication.

AI & Business Automation

Reduce repetitive work with suitable tool connections and AI-supported tasks, with human control where it matters.
