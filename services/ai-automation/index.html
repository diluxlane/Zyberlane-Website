Less repetitive work.
Practical automation.

Zyberlane provides AI assistants, model integrations and business workflow automation remotely. Start with a specific task, the information it needs and the decisions that should stay with a person.

Choose the right kind of automation

01

AI assistants and agents

Customer-support assistants and scoped AI agents can be considered where a model is useful. The requirements need to identify the questions or tasks, suitable information sources and where human review belongs.

02

AI in an existing product

Discuss connecting AI models to a website or application. Data access, the limits of the output and the surrounding workflow are considered before a build is agreed.

03

Business automation & integrations

Connect forms, APIs and third-party services for notifications, routine processes and moving information between systems. A defined rule or connection may be enough without adding AI.

Define inputs, actions and review

Tell us what starts the task, which systems or data it uses and what should happen next. For example, a form submission may need to enter an existing workflow or trigger a notification. Share an example of the task when discussing your requirements.

For an AI assistant, explain the information it may use, the output you expect and what a person needs to review. Access to data, limitations and human review are part of scoping; automated output is not guaranteed to be error-free.

From repeated task to scoped build

  1. 01

    Explain the current process

    Share one task, the tools involved, example inputs and the outcome you need. Explain what is repetitive and where errors or exceptions need attention.

  2. 02

    Review feasibility

    Discuss data access, available integrations and the boundary between automation and human decisions with Dilux. Agree the scope, quotation and timeline before the build.

  3. 03

    Build and review the flow

    Review previews and test the agreed workflow, then arrange delivery. If a custom interface or business tool is needed, consider web application development.

Questions before you automate

01

Does every automation need AI?

No. Form connections, notifications and routine API integrations can follow defined steps. AI assistants are a separate option when the task benefits from model output and appropriate review.

02

Can it connect to existing tools?

We connect APIs and third-party services. Whether a particular tool can be connected depends on access, its integration options and the project requirements.

03

How is automation priced?

Requirements, functionality and integration complexity determine the quotation. Describe the workflow and existing tools first. Confirm any third-party service costs and ongoing support within the project scope.

Tell us what you need to build.

Share your goals, existing tools or content, and the features that matter. Zyberlane works remotely, with direct founder communication from scope to delivery.
