Audit date: 30 October 2026. Base: `909f0077a5a7051ec60783528549efc0dfef8973` on `main`. Branch: `feat/zyberlane-agency-repositioning`. This is a content/business architecture update, not an additional SEO phase or a visual redesign.

## 1. New positioning

Zyberlane is presented as a founder-led web design, branding and digital marketing agency open to remote projects worldwide. The message starts with a clearer business identity, stronger online presence, relevant customer reach and easier enquiries. Websites, search foundations and suitable digital systems are supported by existing work; creative and marketing delivery is confirmed against suitable project-based specialist availability before acceptance.

## 17. Visual QA

The available cloud browser rendered Home, Services, About, Work and Divine Tattoo at fixed iframe widths **320, 390, 768, 1280 and 1440** (25 page/width combinations). After fixes, each document's scroll width matched its client width: 305, 375, 753, 1265 and 1425 respectively, accounting for the browser's 15px scrollbar. There was no page-level horizontal overflow.

Representative screenshots were inspected for the mobile hero, narrow service heading/cards, mobile filters/project card, tablet services, wide About and desktop hero. Browser interactions verified mobile menu open/Escape, expanded paid-ads FAQ, Design Studies/Concept filter results and labels, and native horizontal Why-card keyboard scrolling after focusing the rail. A 320px footer overflow was fixed by allowing the longer agency tagline to wrap. A stylesheet-order issue preventing the Why rail from scrolling was found and corrected; its vertical overflow is hidden so unrevealed cards do not produce an unwanted vertical scrollbar.

[Desktop hero evidence](qa/agency-hero-1280.jpg). Temporary QA wrappers exist only in local `dist`; they are excluded from source and are removed by the next clean build. These are fixed-width cloud-browser checks, not physical-device touch/GPU tests, a full no-JavaScript browser matrix, pixel equivalence or CWV measurements. The three local browser suites remain blocked as described above. No inquiry was submitted, no ad/campaign was created and no authenticated CMS operations were tested. A sampled browser console showed extension errors; no first-party error was shown in that sample.
