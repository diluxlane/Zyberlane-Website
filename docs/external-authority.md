## Google Business Profile eligibility check

Google's [representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) require accurate real-world names and address/service-area information. Its [eligibility rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036) require in-person customer contact; online-only businesses are ineligible. A service-area profile applies to businesses that travel to customers, and the address should be hidden when customers are not served there. The owner should establish the actual operating model before changing fields: whether customers visit a staffed location, Zyberlane visits them in person, or work is entirely remote. Hiding an address does not by itself make an online-only business eligible. Do not add a virtual office, invented location, hours, or second listing.

The Maps destination title contains descriptive service words after “Zyberlane.” The owner should check whether that is the actual real-world business name; Google says service and location details belong in other profile fields, not in an embellished business name.

## Ethical review request

After a real project, invite every eligible client to leave **honest feedback**, including those who expressed concerns. Use the direct link or QR code generated inside the owner's Google Business Profile, via a personal email, WhatsApp message or normal handoff. Do not offer money, discounts, gifts or free services; require no rating, prewritten wording or five stars; do not filter requests to only happy customers or suppress criticism. Never use fake accounts or rewrite a customer's words as their own. A single personal request after completion is sufficient; do not bulk-automate follow-ups.

Suggested neutral wording for the owner to adapt: “Thank you for working with Zyberlane. If you would like to share your experience, you can leave an honest Google review here: [owner-generated review link]. Your feedback helps others understand our work.” Do not substitute the public Maps listing link for an unverified direct review-request link.

Sources: [Google review link/QR instructions](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815), [Maps fake engagement policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114), [rating manipulation policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/16597280).

### Directory shortlist (research candidates, no submissions)

1. [Clutch](https://clutch.co/get-listed): active buyer directory for web/software providers, including a [Sri Lanka web-development category](https://clutch.co/lk/web-developers). Its Basic listing is free but currently states **no website link from directory pages**, so it should be considered for buyer discovery, not promised as a backlink. Verification/paid plans are separate. Use only truthful size, location and real project material; skip if the fields cannot accurately represent a founder-led studio.
2. [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed): active B2B service directory with a free application and research verification, not guaranteed acceptance. Relevant only if the owner can accurately complete the company and project fields; no paid visibility plan is needed for this phase. Do not assume a free listing produces a followed backlink or leads.

No Sri Lanka-specific directory was verified here as both relevant and suitable for a remote-first studio without a required physical address. Do not submit to random directories just to increase a citation count.

Google [link spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) and Bing [link-building guidance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/link-building-7a3f99b7) favor genuine, relevant links over manipulative patterns. Avoid backlink packages, PBNs, spam directories, hidden or keyword-stuffed client credits, three-way exchanges, comment/forum signatures and fake Reddit/Quora recommendations. In real community participation, disclose affiliation when relevant and link only when it helps answer the question.
