About Zyberlane ● Software · Web · AI · Automation

Practical technology.
Purposeful builds.

Zyberlane is an emerging technology company building software, web experiences, AI systems and automation around real needs.

What drives the work

We work with businesses, startups, creators and organizations to turn ideas into useful digital products. The focus is on understanding the problem, choosing a suitable approach and building something people can actually use.

AI-assisted workflows support development and iteration, alongside human decisions, testing and review. Clear communication and a realistic scope are part of the build.

Zyberlane operates remotely from Sri Lanka and can collaborate with clients elsewhere when the project is a suitable fit.
